Field Manual

Command

You are the unseen hand above five empires. Every faction is playable. Click a flag or press 1–5 to assume command of that nation. Its army stops taking AI orders and answers to you.

  • Left mouse: select a unit, drag to box-select.
  • Right mouse: move or attack.
  • WASD / arrows: pan. Wheel: zoom.
  • H: dig in. S: stop. Q: artillery.

The Front

Capture sectors by standing inside them with infantry or cavalry. More units capture faster. A sector must be cleared of its owner's troops before it can fall.

Hold 11 of 15 sectors for 42 seconds to force an armistice. Capture an enemy capital to knock that empire out of the war.

War Economy

Each sector generates requisition. Spend it on five regimental cards. A full field army cannot recruit more; take ground to raise the cap.

Entrenched troops take far less damage. Shells are slow but can break a trench. Use artillery on clusters, then send infantry to take the crater.

Touch

Drag with one finger to pan, pinch to zoom. Tap a unit to select it; tap ground to order it forward. Tap an enemy while troops are selected to attack. The bottom bar carries dig, stop and clear orders.
