1944 — Grand Strategy of the Second World War

Five powers march on the same burning continent. Command your chosen nation — or all five in the sandbox — forge alliances (or betray them), fortify the roads with fifty kinds of field works, and grind the enemy's spearheads into the mud.

CAMPAIGN MODE
COMMAND DIFFICULTY

Orders & Selection

  • Left-click select · Drag box-select
  • Shift add to selection · Double-click all of type on screen
  • Right-click move / attack / capture point
  • A attack-move · S stop · H hold position (toggle)
  • Right-click with a factory selected sets its rally point
  • Touch: tap selects & commands · pinch zooms · two-finger pan

Camera

  • Arrow keys / screen edge — pan
  • Wheel zoom · Q / E rotate

Production & Building

  • Select your HQ → tabs open 50 structures (Fortify · Guns · Support · Camp)
  • B Barracks · F War Factory — click map to place (green ring = valid)
  • Select a producer → Q / W trains units
  • Fortresses: sandbag lines, bunkers, MG nests, anti-tank pits, minefields
  • Fire Direction Centers & Watchtowers supercharge your guns
  • Capture flags A–E for +3 REQ/s each

Objective

  • Destroy every hostile headquarters to win
  • Lose your HQ — and your allies cannot save you
  • At the 15-minute whistle, the stronger HQ claims the field
  • Sandbox: no clock, no defeat — pure creation
